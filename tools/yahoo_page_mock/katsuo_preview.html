This Japanese food product is made with bonito from Japan and finished with a savory soy sauce and dashi-style flavor. Its tender, flaky texture and rich umami make it easy to enjoy as part of an everyday meal.

Open the package and enjoy it as it is, or serve it over warm rice. It also works well with ochazuke, rice balls, salads, chilled tofu, pasta, or as a savory snack with drinks. No complicated preparation is required.

Net weight: 50 g per package
Storage: Keep at room temperature away from direct sunlight, high temperatures, and humidity. After opening, seal well and consume as soon as possible.
